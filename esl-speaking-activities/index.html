Why a daily routine works

Quick speaking prompts you can use today

Describe your perfect weekend in 60 seconds.
Talk about something you learned recently and how you learned it.
Would you rather live in a big city or a small village? Give two reasons.
Explain how to make your favourite snack, step by step.
Tell a partner about a time something didn't go to plan.
