SPEAKING

Speak more. Worry less.

Use these activities aloud. You can practise alone, record yourself, or practise with another person.

30 SECONDS

Quick answer

Describe a place you enjoy visiting. Say where it is, what you do there and why you like it.

OPINION

Give your view

Is it better to study alone or with other people? Give two reasons and one example.

In my opinion… / One reason is… / For example… / However…
ROLE-PLAY

Real-life situation

You bought something online and it arrived damaged. Explain the problem and ask politely for a solution.

1. Explain what happened. 2. Say what you expected. 3. Ask for a solution. 4. Thank the person.

Speaking challenge ladder

A — Answer
Odpowiedz na pytanie pełnym zdaniem.
B — Build
Dodaj powód, przykład lub szczegół.
C — Connect
Połącz pomysły słowami: however, because, for example, although.
