Suggested teaching flow

1 · Warm-up

Activate prior knowledge and personalise the topic.

2 · Language focus

Teach meaning, form, pronunciation and useful chunks.

3 · Input

Use reading or listening to notice language in context.

4 · Production

Move to speaking or writing so the learner produces language.

5 · Check

Record one strength and one next step.

Matura-focused checklist

Speaking

Answer directly, develop the idea, justify an opinion and react to follow-ups.

Writing

Check relevance, organisation, cohesion, language range and register.

Reading & listening

Look for gist, details, paraphrase, inference and evidence.
