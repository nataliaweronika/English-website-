Suggested teaching flow

1 · Warm-up

Activate prior knowledge. Ask personal questions before correcting.

2 · Language focus

Teach meaning, form, pronunciation and useful chunks in context.

3 · Input

Use the reading or listening task to notice target language.

4 · Production

Move to speaking or writing so the learner uses the language independently.

5 · Check & homework

Check understanding, record one strength and one next step.

Matura-focused teacher checklist

Speaking

Can the student answer directly, develop the idea, justify an opinion and react to a follow-up?

Writing

Is the response relevant, organised, cohesive and appropriate for the required register?

Reading

Can the student identify gist, details, paraphrase and evidence rather than relying on single-word matches?
